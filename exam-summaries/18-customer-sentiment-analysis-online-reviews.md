# 18. Customer Sentiment Analysis from Online Reviews

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** Customer Sentiment Analysis from Online Reviews
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study-2`
- **Source filename:** `Glades Magdy - Customer_Sentiment_Analysis_Final (3).pdf`

## 2. Business Problem
Organizations receive large volumes of unstructured reviews that cannot be analyzed manually at scale. Without systematic interpretation, customer pain points and satisfaction trends remain hidden.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The case applies NLP sentiment analysis to classify review polarity and extract actionable opinion signals. It discusses a spectrum from rule-based methods to ML/deep models, including embeddings and confidence scoring.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
Expected value includes faster voice-of-customer monitoring, better product/service prioritization, and evidence-based marketing/service decisions. IS value increases when sentiment outputs feed CRM dashboards and response workflows.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
This case study uses AI to transform customer text into managerial insight. The business problem is that online reviews are high-volume and unstructured, so manual reading is slow and inconsistent. Sentiment analysis provides scalable classification of opinions (positive, neutral, negative) and helps detect patterns by topic, product, or period. The strategic benefit is decision speed: managers can identify dissatisfaction drivers early and prioritize corrective actions. From a digital-transformation angle, sentiment analytics should connect to CRM and service processes, not remain a standalone report. When integrated well, it becomes a continuous feedback system linking customer voice to operational and commercial improvement.

## 6. Keywords
Sentiment analysis, NLP, opinion mining, review analytics, customer insight, CRM feedback loop
