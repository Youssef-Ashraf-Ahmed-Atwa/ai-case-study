# 05. AI-Optimized Inventory Management in Automotive Dealerships

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** AI-Optimized Inventory Management in Automotive Dealerships
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study`
- **Source filename:** `AI-Optimized Inventory Management in Automotive Dealerships-Youssef-Ashraf.pdf`

## 2. Business Problem
Aftersales departments must balance many spare-part SKUs under uncertain demand. Stockouts create service delays and idle bays, while overstock locks working capital and increases obsolescence risk.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The case applies AI-driven demand sensing and replenishment logic to classify parts by demand behavior, prioritize critical items, and improve reorder decisions versus static rules. It reframes inventory from periodic manual control to data-guided optimization.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
Expected gains include better service availability, lower capital tied in slow-moving stock, and improved planning discipline. The systems link is strong: AI recommendations should feed DMS/ERP purchasing and workshop scheduling processes.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
This project addresses a classic aftersales trade-off: too little inventory hurts service speed, while too much inventory hurts cash and profitability. The AI approach uses historical movement patterns and operational context to improve stocking decisions at SKU level. Compared with traditional min-max routines, the model can adapt faster to demand shifts and part criticality. Business value appears in both revenue protection (fewer missed repairs) and financial efficiency (lower excess stock). For information systems, the critical success factor is integration—AI outputs must update procurement, stock control, and service planning workflows in ERP/DMS environments. That integration makes inventory management a practical digital-transformation capability rather than a standalone analytics experiment.

## 6. Keywords
Spare parts, inventory optimization, demand forecasting, stockout reduction, working capital, DMS/ERP
