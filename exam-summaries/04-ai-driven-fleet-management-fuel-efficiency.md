# 04. AI-Driven Fleet Management and Fuel Efficiency Optimization

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** AI-Driven Fleet Management and Fuel Efficiency Optimization
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study`
- **Source filename:** `AI-Driven Fleet Management & Fuel Efficiency Optimization.pdf`

## 2. Business Problem
Transport fleets lose money through fuel waste, idle time, non-optimized driving behavior, and reactive maintenance. Data exists across telematics and operations, but it is not used proactively in dispatch decisions.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The case proposes a multi-layer AI architecture using IoT/telematics signals (GPS, speed, fuel, vehicle diagnostics), predictive models for inefficiency/failure risk, and automated actions linked to dispatch and maintenance workflows.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
The expected value is lower fuel consumption, reduced downtime, and better asset utilization. ERP/RPA linkage is central because predictions must trigger maintenance tickets, alerts, and route adjustments inside operational systems.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
The fleet case demonstrates how AI converts raw telematics into operational decisions. The business problem is high logistics cost driven by avoidable fuel burn and unplanned failures. Instead of retrospective reporting, the proposed system continuously analyzes IoT data to detect inefficient driving patterns, idle losses, and maintenance risk. These insights become valuable only when connected to execution layers such as dispatch planning and ERP-maintenance workflows. In exam terms, the key argument is that AI supports both cost and reliability strategy: it reduces waste today while improving service consistency tomorrow. This is a clear digital-transformation pattern—moving from reactive fleet control to predictive, system-integrated logistics management.

## 6. Keywords
Fleet analytics, fuel optimization, telematics, predictive maintenance, dispatch automation, ERP integration
