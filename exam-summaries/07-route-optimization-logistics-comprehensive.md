## 2. Business Problem
Static route planning cannot respond quickly to traffic volatility, demand variation, and service-level pressure. The result is excess fuel cost, delays, and avoidable operational risk.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The comprehensive project proposes real-time AI routing supported by forecasting, risk scoring, and continuous retraining (MLOps mindset). It also includes automated orchestration where detected risks trigger workflow actions across transport systems.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
Expected value includes lower transport cost, better on-time performance, and faster exception handling. ERP/TMS integration and RPA triggers are central to translating predictions into operational execution.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
In this route-optimization case, AI is used to replace static planning with adaptive decisioning. The business problem is multi-dimensional: costs rise from congestion and poor routing, while service reliability declines when plans cannot adapt in real time. The solution combines predictive analytics with routing optimization and process automation so that disruptions trigger immediate operational responses. A major strategic point is lifecycle management: routing models must be retrained continuously as demand and traffic patterns change. This links AI practice to digital-transformation governance, not one-time deployment. When integrated with TMS/ERP and dispatch workflows, the approach improves both efficiency and responsiveness, which are key competitive factors in logistics.
