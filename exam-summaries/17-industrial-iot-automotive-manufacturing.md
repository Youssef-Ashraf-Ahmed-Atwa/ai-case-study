# 17. Industrial IoT in Automotive Manufacturing (GB Auto Prima Factory)

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** Industrial IoT in Automotive Manufacturing (GB Auto Prima Factory)
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study`
- **Source filename:** `GB_Auto_Prima_Factory_IIoT_Project_25092026.pdf`

## 2. Business Problem
Factory utility and production monitoring is limited by coarse metering and noisy operational data, making cost attribution and process optimization difficult at sub-process level.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The case proposes an Industry 4.0 architecture combining IIoT sensing, edge/cloud analytics, data correction methods, and predictive capabilities to improve operational visibility and maintenance planning.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
Expected value is better cost transparency, stronger process control, and improved reliability of decision data. As digital transformation, the project connects OT signals with enterprise analytics/ERP governance for plant-level optimization.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
This case illustrates a foundational digital-manufacturing challenge: you cannot optimize what you cannot measure accurately. With limited and noisy metering, management cannot reliably attribute utility cost or detect inefficiency by line or process. The IIoT approach introduces richer sensing and analytics to convert raw industrial signals into trustworthy operational intelligence. That enables better maintenance planning, process tuning, and cost governance. The broader business lesson is integration between operational technology and enterprise systems: data quality engineering, not only AI modeling, is essential for value. In exam framing, this project is a practical Industry 4.0 transition from fragmented monitoring to data-driven manufacturing control.

## 6. Keywords
IIoT, Industry 4.0, manufacturing analytics, edge-cloud architecture, data quality, OT-IT integration
