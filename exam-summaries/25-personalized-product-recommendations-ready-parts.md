# 25. AI Personalized Product Recommendations (Ready Parts)

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** AI Personalized Product Recommendations (Ready Parts)
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study-2`
- **Source filename:** `Ready Parts AI Personalized Product Recommendations.pdf`

## 2. Business Problem
In automotive e-commerce, customers may find a searched item but miss compatible complementary parts, reducing basket value and weakening shopping guidance.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The case proposes a fitment-first hybrid recommender combining behavior, product similarity, business rules, stock status, and compatibility constraints. It is based on a synthetic academic dataset and governed pilot approach.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
Expected value is stronger cross-sell and discovery while protecting trust by suppressing incompatible or unavailable recommendations. IS value depends on integrating recommendation logic with product, inventory, and transaction data models.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
This recommendation case balances commercial growth with technical trust. The business challenge is to increase relevant cross-sell without suggesting wrong parts, which is critical in fitment-sensitive automotive catalogs. The AI solution combines multiple ranking signals and explicitly prioritizes compatibility and availability constraints. That design choice is strategically important: recommendation quality is measured by incremental business value and customer confidence, not clicks alone. The case also highlights data architecture and controlled experimentation, showing how recommendation systems should be deployed through governed pilots. In digital-transformation terms, it turns product discovery into a data-driven capability connected to core e-commerce and inventory systems.

## 6. Keywords
Recommender systems, fitment-first logic, cross-sell, automotive e-commerce, compatibility constraints, governed pilot
