# 03. AI-Assisted Medical Diagnosis in Hospitals (Final Report Version)

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** AI-Assisted Medical Diagnosis in Hospitals (Final Report Version)
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study`
- **Source filename:** `AI-Assisted Medical Diagnosis in Hospital-Final Report.pdf`

## 2. Business Problem
Hospitals face diagnostic delays and variability because clinicians must process large volumes of heterogeneous patient data under time pressure. Manual-only workflows make consistency difficult and increase operational burden.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The report frames an AI-assisted decision-support architecture using clinical data, classification models, and workflow integration (including RPA elements). The model supports risk/diagnosis prioritization while keeping physicians as final decision-makers.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
Expected benefits include faster triage, more consistent decision support, and better utilization of clinical staff time. From a digital-transformation view, value depends on embedding AI into HIS/EMR processes with governance, model monitoring, and accountability controls.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
This case study positions AI as clinical decision support, not physician replacement. The core problem is that diagnosis quality and speed are constrained by data overload and manual interpretation. The proposed approach uses machine-learning classification on structured clinical signals to highlight likely risks or diagnostic directions early. Operationally, this can reduce delays and help doctors focus on complex judgment rather than repetitive data scanning. The business and IS lesson is that healthcare AI succeeds only when integrated with hospital information flows and governance: data quality, model evaluation, and clear human accountability must remain central. Therefore, AI becomes an enabler of safer, more scalable hospital operations within a broader digital-health transformation.

## 6. Keywords
Clinical decision support, hospital AI, classification models, physician augmentation, RPA workflow, healthcare digital transformation
