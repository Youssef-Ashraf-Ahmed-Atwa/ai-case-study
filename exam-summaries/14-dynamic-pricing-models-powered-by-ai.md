# 14. Dynamic Pricing Models Powered by AI (Passenger Car Spare Parts)

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** Dynamic Pricing Models Powered by AI (Passenger Car Spare Parts)
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study`
- **Source filename:** `Dynamic Pricing Models Powered by AI #Haytham.pdf`

## 2. Business Problem
Static pricing in spare-parts markets cannot react quickly to demand shifts, competitor moves, and inventory conditions. This leads to missed margin opportunities on one side and lost sales on the other.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The case positions AI-based dynamic pricing as a continuous optimization process that updates price recommendations using demand signals, elasticity patterns, and commercial constraints. Pricing becomes data-driven rather than purely rule-based.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
Expected value includes better margin management, faster market response, and improved stock monetization. In ERP terms, pricing intelligence must integrate with catalog, inventory, and order-management systems to be operationally viable.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
This dynamic-pricing case addresses a core commercial challenge: one fixed price cannot maximize value across changing market conditions. In automotive spare parts, demand volatility and competitive pressure require faster pricing adaptation than manual cycles can provide. AI models can estimate likely demand response and generate contextual price recommendations within policy boundaries. The business objective is balanced optimization—protect profitability while maintaining competitiveness and availability. For information systems, the key is execution integration: recommended prices must flow into ERP and sales channels with governance, approval logic, and monitoring. As a digital-transformation initiative, the project shifts pricing from periodic administrative updates to an always-learning commercial capability.

## 6. Keywords
Dynamic pricing, price optimization, demand elasticity, margin management, spare parts, ERP pricing workflow
