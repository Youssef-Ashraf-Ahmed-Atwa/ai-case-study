# 12. Demand Forecasting and Inventory Management with ML in Retail Chains

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** Demand Forecasting and Inventory Management with ML in Retail Chains
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study`
- **Source filename:** `Demand Forecasting & Inventory Management with Machine Learning in Retail Chains Final.pdf`

## 2. Business Problem
Retail distributors with long lead times and many SKUs struggle with judgment-based forecasting, causing stockouts, excess inventory, and weak service levels under volatile market conditions.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The case applies SKU-level machine-learning forecasting, segmentation, back-testing, and policy redesign, then links outputs to replenishment and transfer decisions. It uses a hybrid implementation model combining data science cycles and ERP stage-gates.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
The case reports measurable KPI baselines and improvements in forecast/service/inventory outcomes. Systemically, value is amplified because one forecast layer feeds cross-functional ERP processes (sales, finance, warehouse, procurement).

## 5. Exam-ready answer
This project shows how forecasting quality drives enterprise performance beyond inventory alone. The problem is structural: long lead times and intermittent demand make manual planning unreliable. The AI/ML approach selects models per SKU behavior, validates them through back-testing, and connects predictions to replenishment and allocation rules. The business result is better fill-rate and lower inventory distortion, with benefits shared across operations and finance. The key exam insight is integration: AI forecasting becomes transformative only when embedded in ERP decision flows and planner routines, with governance for overrides and retraining. That turns forecasting from a monthly report into a continuous decision engine supporting resilient retail operations.

## 6. Keywords
Demand forecasting, SKU segmentation, ML back-testing, replenishment policy, ERP integration, supply-chain resilience
