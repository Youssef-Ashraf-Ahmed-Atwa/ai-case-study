# 28. AI-Powered Voice and Speech Analytics in Call Centers

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** AI-Powered Voice and Speech Analytics in Call Centers
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study-2`
- **Source filename:** `Task4_Voice_Speech_Analytics_FINAL.pptx`

## 2. Business Problem
Call centers generate large volumes of voice interactions, but manual quality monitoring captures only a small sample and delays insight on customer issues and agent performance.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The project proposes speech analytics using transcription, sentiment/emotion/topic signals, and KPI frameworks from descriptive to predictive use. It notes synthetic dataset usage and phased project development.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
Expected value is improved customer-experience management, better coaching precision, and faster issue detection. Digital-transformation relevance includes governance for recorded-voice data, consent, and integration with CRM/service operations.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
This case applies AI to one of the richest but least-structured service data sources: call audio. The business problem is visibility—manual QA cannot scale to full interaction volume, so performance and customer-risk signals arrive too late. Speech analytics can convert conversations into structured indicators such as sentiment, topics, compliance cues, and operational KPIs. That enables earlier intervention for customer pain points and more targeted agent coaching. The case also highlights governance: voice data requires strict privacy and consent controls. In a digital-transformation frame, the initiative moves contact-center management from sample-based review to continuous, data-driven service intelligence integrated with core customer systems.

## 6. Keywords
Speech analytics, call center AI, transcription, sentiment and topic detection, QA automation, CX intelligence
