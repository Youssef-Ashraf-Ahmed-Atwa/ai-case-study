# 16. AI-Powered Talent Intelligence and Internal Mobility at GB Corp

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** AI-Powered Talent Intelligence and Internal Mobility at GB Corp
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study`
- **Source filename:** `GB Corp Talent Intelligence — Final Presentation.pdf`

## 2. Business Problem
Large diversified groups often hire externally for roles that internal employees could fill because skill information is trapped in unstructured CVs and inconsistent records.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The case uses NLP-based skill extraction and transparent scoring to map employee capabilities to role requirements and identify internal mobility options. It emphasizes explainability and fair decision support.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
Expected value includes lower external hiring dependency, faster staffing decisions, and stronger workforce development ROI. Information-system value depends on integrating HR datasets, talent workflows, and governance controls.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
This project tackles a strategic HR inefficiency: invisible internal capability. The business problem is not lack of talent but lack of structured insight into available skills across business units. AI helps by extracting and standardizing skill signals from CVs, job descriptions, and performance/training records, then matching candidates to roles through explainable scoring. The value proposition is both economic and organizational—reduced recruitment cost, faster placement, and stronger retention through visible career paths. From an IS perspective, talent intelligence is a digital-transformation layer for HR, where data standardization, fairness checks, and manager oversight are as important as model accuracy.

## 6. Keywords
Talent intelligence, internal mobility, HR analytics, NLP skill extraction, explainable scoring, workforce transformation
