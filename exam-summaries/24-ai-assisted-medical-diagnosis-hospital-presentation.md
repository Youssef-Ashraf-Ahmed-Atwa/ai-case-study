# 24. AI-Assisted Medical Diagnosis in Hospitals (Presentation Version)

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** AI-Assisted Medical Diagnosis in Hospitals (Presentation Version)
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study-2`
- **Source filename:** `Presentation-AI-Assisted Medical Diagnosis in Hospital.pptx`

## 2. Business Problem
The presentation emphasizes physician workload and diagnostic-process variability in data-intensive hospital settings, where manual review makes speed and consistency difficult.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
It proposes AI-assisted clinical insight as a decision-support layer that organizes patient information and provides diagnostic support signals while preserving physician authority over final decisions.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
Expected value is reduced information-processing burden, faster decision support, and more consistent diagnostic pathways. The transformation perspective is human-centered AI integrated into hospital workflows.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
Compared with purely technical AI narratives, this case explicitly centers human judgment. The business problem is that clinicians face increasing information complexity and time pressure, which can slow diagnosis and increase variation. AI is positioned as an assistive layer that helps process data and highlight relevant insights, not a replacement for doctors. This framing supports adoption because it aligns technology with professional accountability and care quality. For exam analysis, the key linkage is operational: value emerges when AI recommendations are embedded into routine clinical workflow, with governance on data quality, oversight, and interpretation standards. That is the digital-transformation logic behind sustainable healthcare AI.

## 6. Keywords
Physician augmentation, diagnostic consistency, clinical workflow, decision support, healthcare AI adoption, human-centered AI
