# 13. Drug Discovery and Development Using AI (Regeneron Case)

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** Drug Discovery and Development Using AI (Regeneron Case)
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study`
- **Source filename:** `Drug Discovery Using AI Regeneron Company.pptx`

## 2. Business Problem
Drug development is expensive, slow, and failure-prone, and traditional preclinical assumptions often translate poorly to human outcomes. Algorithms alone do not solve this without high-quality biological data.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The case emphasizes AI-driven target discovery anchored in large-scale human genetic/clinical datasets. It argues that competitive advantage comes from data assets plus model capability, not model capability alone.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
Expected value is shortened discovery cycles, better target prioritization, and reduced late-stage failure risk. For digital strategy, this case links AI value to enterprise data platforms, governance, and translational research workflows.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
In this pharma case, AI is framed as a scientific acceleration tool constrained by data quality. The business problem is the high cost and low success rate of traditional drug discovery pipelines. The proposed logic is to start from human biological evidence, then use AI models to identify and prioritize targets with stronger probability of clinical relevance. This improves decision quality early, where failure is cheapest to prevent. The strategic lesson is that algorithms are increasingly accessible; durable advantage depends on proprietary, well-governed data ecosystems and integration between research, analytics, and development operations. Therefore, AI contributes to both innovation productivity and portfolio risk management in biopharma.
