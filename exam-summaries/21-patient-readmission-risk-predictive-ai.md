# 21. Patient Readmission Risk (Predictive AI Program)

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** Patient Readmission Risk (Predictive AI Program)
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study-2`
- **Source filename:** `Patient Readmission Risk - AI Case Study.pdf`

## 2. Business Problem
Hospitals need to reduce avoidable 30-day readmissions that increase cost pressure and indicate care-continuity gaps. Manual identification of high-risk patients is limited and late.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The case builds a structured predictive workflow on patient-level data, including cleaning, feature preparation, and supervised risk modeling (baseline logistic plus boosting-based scoring), to identify high-risk discharges for intervention.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
Expected value is better targeting of post-discharge programs and more efficient care-management resource allocation. Information-system impact comes from embedding risk scores into discharge planning and follow-up workflows.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
The readmission case demonstrates predictive AI for proactive care management. The core business problem is that hospitals often detect risk after readmission occurs, when intervention value is lost. By using patient data to estimate readmission probability at discharge, the hospital can prioritize follow-up actions for patients most likely to return. This improves operational focus and supports quality goals. The case also shows good analytics discipline through explicit data-quality preparation and model-role separation. In digital-transformation terms, the true value comes when risk scores are operationalized in care pathways, not when they remain in analytical dashboards only.

## 6. Keywords
Readmission prediction, healthcare ML, risk stratification, discharge planning, care management, clinical analytics
