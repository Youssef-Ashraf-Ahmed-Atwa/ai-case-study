# 11. AI-Enabled Construction Inspection-to-Invoice Reconciliation

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** AI-Enabled Construction Inspection-to-Invoice Reconciliation
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study`
- **Source filename:** `Construction_Cash_Gap_Framework_Report Final (Ahmed Hassan Asfahany).pdf`

## 2. Business Problem
EPC and infrastructure projects often rely on manual sample-based reconciliation between site progress and contractor invoices. This creates slow cycles, limited review coverage, and overbilling risk.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The framework combines data capture, AI/analytics verification, RPA execution, and ERP posting controls to validate billed quantities against verified work before payment. The case explicitly treats governance and auditability as design requirements.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
The report states major performance improvement in review coverage and reconciliation cycle time in its representative scenario. Beyond numbers, the business value is stronger financial control, trust, and scalable governance across sites.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
This case reframes reconciliation as a control-system problem, not only an accounting task. The business challenge is the gap between what is built on site and what is invoiced in ERP workflows. Manual review cannot scale, so risk remains hidden until late. The AI-enabled framework introduces structured verification before payment by combining inspection evidence, model-based matching, and automated workflow routing. Its strategic importance lies in responsible automation: human judgment is preserved for exceptions, while routine checks are standardized and auditable. In IS/ERP terms, it is a high-impact digital-transformation pattern where AI improves both speed and financial governance in project-based enterprises.

## 6. Keywords
EPC reconciliation, overbilling control, AI verification, RPA workflow, ERP governance, auditable automation
