# 02. AI Computer Vision in Automotive Service Centers

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** AI Computer Vision in Automotive Service Centers
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study`
- **Source filename:** `AI Computer Vision in Automotive Service Centers -kerolos_Magdy.pdf`

## 2. Business Problem
Service centers often rely on manual inspection for tire or component condition assessment. This creates inconsistency between technicians, slows intake, and limits traceability of inspection quality across branches.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The case uses computer vision to classify captured images (for example safe vs worn condition) and generate confidence scores per inspection. Model outputs are logged into structured records, creating a repeatable inspection data stream for analytics and quality control.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
The expected value is faster intake, more consistent decisions, and auditable inspection records. In IS terms, image AI becomes a front-line decision-support service feeding workshop systems, inventory planning, and customer communication workflows.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
This project shows how computer vision can standardize inspection work in automotive service operations. Instead of depending only on technician judgment, the system analyzes intake images and returns both a class label and confidence score. That output can be stored as structured data and used in reports, escalation rules, and service recommendations. The business problem is not only speed; it is consistency and accountability. AI reduces variance between branches and gives managers measurable evidence of inspection quality. When integrated with workshop and parts systems, the model supports better service planning and customer trust, which links directly to digital transformation in aftersales operations.

## 6. Keywords
Computer vision, inspection automation, confidence scoring, service-center analytics, quality consistency, workshop systems
