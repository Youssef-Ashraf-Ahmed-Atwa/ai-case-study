# 10. Chatbots for Customer Service Automation in Retail Banking

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** Chatbots for Customer Service Automation in Retail Banking
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study`
- **Source filename:** `Chatbots for Customer Service Automation.pptx`

## 2. Business Problem
Retail banking customers expect instant service across digital channels, but call centers and branch-based support create delays and high handling costs for repetitive inquiries.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The project frames conversational AI chatbots as an automation layer for routine requests, with escalation paths for complex cases. It emphasizes business understanding and integration variability across banking systems.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
Expected outcomes are faster response times, improved availability, and lower service workload pressure. The IS link is strong because chatbot value depends on secure integration with core banking, CRM, and service-ticket systems.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
The banking chatbot case focuses on service scalability under rising digital expectations. The business problem is that traditional support channels cannot provide always-on, low-latency responses at sustainable cost. Conversational AI can automate high-volume routine requests and provide immediate first-line support, while sensitive or complex cases are escalated to human agents. This hybrid design improves customer experience and operational efficiency together. From an information-systems perspective, success requires integration with banking data and process controls so responses are accurate, secure, and auditable. Therefore, the project is a digital-transformation step in customer operations, moving service from channel-centric handling to AI-assisted omnichannel management.

## 6. Keywords
Banking chatbot, conversational AI, customer service automation, omnichannel support, core-system integration, service efficiency
