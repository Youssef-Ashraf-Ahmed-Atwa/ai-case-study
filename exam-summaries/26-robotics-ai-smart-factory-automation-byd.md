# 26. Robotics and AI for Smart Factory Automation (BYD Dark Factory Case)

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** Robotics and AI for Smart Factory Automation (BYD Dark Factory Case)
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study-2`
- **Source filename:** `Robotics and AI for Smart Factory Automation(1).pptx`

## 2. Business Problem
Manufacturing competitiveness depends on throughput, quality, and cost control, but conventional line management struggles with real-time optimization across robotic cells and maintenance risks.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The case presents an AI-orchestrated robotic-factory pipeline covering production execution, vision quality control, anomaly detection, and predictive maintenance, supported by simulation/digital-twin validation before investment decisions.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
Expected value includes improved throughput reliability, quality consistency, and better capital-allocation decisions for automation programs. The IS/ERP angle includes linking operational KPIs with financial and planning models.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
This smart-factory case treats AI as the coordination layer of advanced automation, not only a standalone model. The business problem is balancing speed, quality, and cost in a highly automated production environment. The proposed architecture combines robotic execution data with AI modules for quality inspection, maintenance prediction, and operational rebalancing. Simulation is used to test feasibility and bottlenecks before full rollout, which reduces implementation risk. For exam discussion, the main digital-transformation insight is integration between shop-floor intelligence and enterprise decision frameworks: operational KPIs must feed financial and planning decisions to justify and refine automation strategy.

## 6. Keywords
Smart factory, robotics orchestration, vision quality control, predictive maintenance, digital twin, manufacturing analytics
