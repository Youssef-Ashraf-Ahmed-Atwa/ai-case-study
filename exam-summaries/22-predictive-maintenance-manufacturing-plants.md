# 22. Predictive Maintenance Using AI in Manufacturing Plants

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** Predictive Maintenance Using AI in Manufacturing Plants
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study-2`
- **Source filename:** `Predictive Maintenance Using AI in Manufacturing Plants -Mina Tharwat- 24128528(1).pptx`

## 2. Business Problem
Traditional preventive maintenance is schedule-driven and can cause both unnecessary servicing and missed condition-specific failures, leading to downtime and avoidable maintenance cost.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The project proposes condition-based predictive maintenance using telemetry such as vibration, thermal, acoustic, and oil signals, plus remaining useful life modeling and integration with CMMS work-order processes.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
Expected value includes reduced unplanned breakdowns, better spare-parts planning, and improved asset utilization. Digital-transformation value appears through data-driven maintenance scheduling integrated with plant systems.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
This case shows the shift from time-based maintenance to condition-aware maintenance. The business problem is inefficiency in traditional routines: equipment may be serviced too early, too late, or in ways that introduce new faults. AI predictive maintenance addresses this by continuously analyzing machine-condition signals and estimating failure risk or remaining life. Operations can then prioritize interventions where they create the highest reliability impact. The business value combines lower downtime, stronger throughput stability, and better working-capital use in spare parts. From an IS perspective, the model must integrate with CMMS and production planning workflows so predictions become executable maintenance actions.

## 6. Keywords
Predictive maintenance, condition monitoring, RUL modeling, CMMS integration, manufacturing reliability, downtime reduction
