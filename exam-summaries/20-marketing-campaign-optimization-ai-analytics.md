# 20. Marketing Campaign Optimization with AI Analytics

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** Marketing Campaign Optimization with AI Analytics
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study-2`
- **Source filename:** `Marketing Campaign Optimization with AI Analytics.pdf`

## 2. Business Problem
Managing many campaigns across countries/channels with spreadsheet reporting causes delayed reaction to underperformance and inefficient budget allocation under rising media costs.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The case proposes AI analytics over campaign data (CTR, CPC, CPA, conversions, ROAS, etc.) to predict outcomes, detect weak performance earlier, and guide budget/channel adjustments.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
Expected value includes improved spend efficiency, faster optimization cycles, and better strategic allocation decisions. IS relevance lies in turning fragmented marketing data into a scalable decision system.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
This case addresses the scaling problem in modern digital marketing: campaign complexity grows faster than manual analysis capacity. The business consequence is late identification of weak campaigns and budget waste. AI analytics can process multi-dimensional performance data continuously, flag at-risk campaigns earlier, and recommend where to reallocate spend. This moves marketing from retrospective reporting to proactive optimization. In business-value terms, the goal is not simply higher traffic but better economic efficiency of acquisition and conversion. From an information-systems perspective, the project demonstrates how analytics infrastructure becomes a competitive capability when integrated with campaign operations and governance.

## 6. Keywords
Campaign analytics, budget optimization, ROAS, predictive marketing, performance monitoring, decision automation
