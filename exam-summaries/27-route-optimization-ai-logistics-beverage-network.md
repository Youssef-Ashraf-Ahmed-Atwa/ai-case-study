# 27. Route Optimization Using AI in Logistics (Beverage Distribution Network)

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** Route Optimization Using AI in Logistics (Beverage Distribution Network)
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study-2`
- **Source filename:** `Route_Optimization_AI_Logistics_ProblemAnalysis.pptx`

## 2. Business Problem
A national beverage distribution network faces seasonal demand spikes, mixed urban/rural routing constraints, fuel inefficiency, and limited real-time visibility, which hurt service reliability and margins.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The case frames AI routing with ML forecasting, clustering for delivery-zone structuring, and automated synchronization of route and proof-of-delivery data with enterprise systems.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
Expected value includes reduced mileage waste, better delivery-window adherence, and faster reconciliation. The IS link is explicit through ERP-connected route confirmation and delivery-data automation.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
This logistics case is distinct because it focuses on a beverage distribution context with strong seasonality and geographic variability. The business problem is operational instability: fixed plans fail under fluctuating demand and traffic realities, increasing cost and reducing service quality. AI supports improvement through predictive planning, route optimization, and zone intelligence based on outlet patterns. It also improves control by automating route and delivery-data synchronization with enterprise records. In exam terms, the key argument is that AI enhances both efficiency and service competitiveness when embedded into end-to-end logistics information flows, not treated as isolated optimization software.

## 6. Keywords
Logistics routing, demand seasonality, delivery optimization, clustering, proof-of-delivery automation, ERP sync
