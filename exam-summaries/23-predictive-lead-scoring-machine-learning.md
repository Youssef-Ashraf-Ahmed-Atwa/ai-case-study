# 23. Predictive Lead Scoring Using Machine Learning

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** Predictive Lead Scoring Using Machine Learning
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study-2`
- **Source filename:** `Predictive_Lead_Scoring_Final_Presentation.pdf`

## 2. Business Problem
Sales teams in education consultancy spend effort on leads with uneven conversion potential. Without predictive prioritization, resource allocation is inefficient and follow-up timing is suboptimal.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The case uses supervised ML to estimate conversion probability per lead, combining structured CRM fields with behavioral signals from notes (NLP-enabled features). It also defines data cleaning and unresolved-case exclusion rules.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
Expected value includes improved sales focus, faster response to high-intent leads, and better conversion productivity per agent. Governance elements (privacy, bias checks, human oversight) are part of the operating design.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
This project frames lead qualification as a prediction problem. The business issue is not lack of leads but lack of prioritization quality under limited sales capacity. By learning from historical conversion behavior, the ML model can score new leads and support sequencing decisions. That helps teams direct effort to prospects with higher expected return while still retaining managerial judgment. The case is strong analytically because it treats data quality and governance explicitly, including unresolved outcomes and fairness risks. In digital-transformation terms, predictive scoring upgrades CRM from passive storage to active decision support in daily sales operations.

## 6. Keywords
Lead scoring, conversion prediction, CRM analytics, NLP features, sales prioritization, responsible AI
