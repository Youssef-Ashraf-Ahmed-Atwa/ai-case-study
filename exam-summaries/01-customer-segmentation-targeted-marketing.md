# 01. AI-Based Customer Segmentation and Targeted Marketing

## 1. Project / Case Study
- **Project title:** AI-Based Customer Segmentation and Targeted Marketing
- **Source repository:** `ai-case-study`
- **Source filename:** `(Final Presentation) AI-Based Customer Segmentation and Targeted Marketing- Report (Ehab Shoukry) ID 24128379.pdf`

## 2. Business Problem
The case addresses a common retail problem: blanket campaigns treat all customers similarly, so marketing budgets are spent on low-response audiences while high-value customers receive weak personalization. The project also highlights fragmented customer data inside ERP and sales systems, which blocks precise targeting.

## 3. AI/ML/Analytics Solution
The case proposes a segmentation pipeline that combines unsupervised clustering (such as K-Means) with supervised models to move from static demographics to behavior-based micro-segments. Transaction history and campaign response data are transformed into segment features, then used to predict the best offer/message for each segment.

## 4. Business Value and Information Systems / ERP / Digital Transformation Link
Business value comes from better campaign fit, lower wasted spend, and stronger retention. From an information-systems perspective, the solution depends on integrating AI outputs with CRM/ERP workflows so segment insights become operational actions, not dashboard-only analytics.

## 5. Exam-ready answer
In this case study, AI is used to solve the inefficiency of mass marketing. The company has rich historical data, but traditional methods cannot convert it into actionable customer groups. By clustering customers based on behavior and then using predictive models to guide campaign decisions, the business can target offers more accurately and improve conversion quality. The strategic point is that AI alone is not enough; impact appears when segmentation outputs are connected to CRM and ERP execution, so marketing teams can automate audience selection and offer timing. This turns marketing from intuition-led planning into data-driven decisioning and supports broader digital transformation through measurable, repeatable campaign optimization.
